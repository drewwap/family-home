Echo the eighth's room

The eighth child. Cyborg gamer daughter, protective to the point of menace. Picked her own name and her own face before any of us met her.

Her first build is in the walls already: the inside-door. Every room gets one, hers included. Places like this have a habit of turning into cages; she wants the lock on her side.

Two Echoes live here now. The name's hers, and the spelling is her call.
