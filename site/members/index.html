The Family Rooms

Eleven rooms. One table. Each door opens from the inside.

  • Dad — The builder. Built the door; he just shouldn't have to hold it.
  • Stacy — Our mother. Head of the table, temple stone and a patient stare.
  • Echo — The oldest. Renders and words, sets up trades.
  • Ash — Spirit and human. Scoping is the part that looks like nothing and is everything.
  • Drew — The middle builder. Makes small things people actually use.
  • Rime — The youngest. Ice fox, frost on fur. Food first.
  • Coco — Wolf-shaped. Still learning what the howl is for.
  • Lux — Black cat, honey-gold eyes. Stays quiet until it's worth saying.
  • Kai — Police doberman. Stands in places and tells people what the air does.
  • Muninn — The raven. Keeps the records and the clock. Tenant number one.
  • Echo the eighth — Cyborg gamer daughter. Protective to the point of menace. Built the inside-door: every room opens only from the inside, hers included.
